FAQ

Good questions

Does Movo record or upload my camera?

No. Pictures are analysed in memory and discarded immediately. There is no code that saves or sends them, and Movo works without internet.

Will it run on my old laptop?

Most likely. Movo needs about 10–20 ms of processor time per frame, rests at 5 frames per second when nobody is in view, and has an Eco mode.

I wear glasses — will eye gestures work?

Yes. Turn on “I wear glasses” and run the 7-second calibration while wearing them. Avoid a lamp reflecting in your lenses.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

That appears because Movo isn't code-signed yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Is there an iPhone version?

Apple doesn't allow apps to control other apps. On iPhone and iPad, Movo controls its own PDF and photo viewer and works as a remote camera for your computer. It isn't on the App Store yet.

Is Movo really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and no data collection. Organisations interested in kiosk, clinic or classroom deployments can get in touch below.
